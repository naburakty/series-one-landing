A FEW FINER DETAILS

Which tools are in the series?+

Series 01 brings together a precision clipper, detail trimmer, twin-foil shaver, and blow dryer. Each tool is imagined in the same four finishes: black, bone white, dark gray, and red.

How does the light indicator work?+

Three indicators illuminate one by one in this scroll sequence. Then the L3VEL3 mark lights up and reveals the rest of the collection.

Can I match the whole collection?+

Yes. Choose a finish anywhere on this page and the entire collection changes together. You can add individual tools to the concept bag in your selected color.

Is this available to purchase?+

This is a design concept. You can explore the product and try the shopping bag, but no orders or payments are taken.
